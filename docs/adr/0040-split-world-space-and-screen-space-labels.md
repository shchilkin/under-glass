# Split world-space and screen-space labels

Group names render as transparent textured planes inside their Group surfaces, while Connection captions render as compact textured nameplates aligned to the longest segment of their route. Both share the scene’s perspective and animate naturally between isometric and top cameras. Connection nameplates use deterministic lanes, resolve world-space collisions, and shrink continuously toward the top camera so their apparent size remains useful in both modes. Node labels remain screen-space HTML overlays because they identify primary entities and need stable size and crisp legibility. The overlay retains visually hidden Group and Connection text for accessibility, while this presentation distinction remains a renderer concern rather than a new Visualization schema field.
